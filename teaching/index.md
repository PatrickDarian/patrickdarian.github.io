# Teaching Experience

## Department Teaching Assistant
*Universitas Indonesia* — Over 6 Semesters of experience across 7 distinct courses

Served as a Department Teaching Assistant across multiple semesters, responsible for developing tutorial papers, grading homework assignments, and conducting tutorial sessions for core undergraduate mathematics courses:

### Calculus
*   **Calculus I** (3 Semesters)  
    *   *Curriculum:* Chapters 1–7 of Purcell's *Calculus* (focusing on limits, derivatives, applications of differentiation, integration, and techniques of integration).
*   **Calculus II** (3 Semesters)
    *   *Curriculum:* Chapters 8–10 and 12-13 of Purcell's *Calculus* (covering indeterminate forms, improper integrals, infinite series, parametric and polar coordinates, multivariable functions, partial derivatives, and multiple integration across rectangular, polar, cylindrical, and spherical coordinates).

### Analysis & Topology
*   **Real Analysis I** (4 Semesters)  
    *   *Curriculum:* Guided students through rigorous foundational analysis spanning Chapters 2–5 of Bartle's *Introduction to Real Analysis* (covering the real number system, sequences, series, limits, and continuous functions).
*   **Real Analysis II** (2 Semesters)  
    *   *Curriculum:* Advanced instruction covering Chapters 5–8 of Bartle's *Introduction to Real Analysis* (focusing on continuity, differentiation, Riemann integration, and sequences of functions).

### Algebraic Structures & Linear Algebra
*   **Elementary Linear Algebra** (1 Semester)  
    *   *Curriculum:* Introductory concepts spanning Chapters 1–3 and 5.1 of Howard Anton's *Elementary Linear Algebra* (covering systems of linear equations, matrices, determinants, Euclidean vector spaces, and Eigenvalues).
*   **Linear Algebra I** (2 Semesters)  
    *   *Curriculum:* Detailed instruction covering Chapters 4–8 of Howard Anton's *Elementary Linear Algebra* (focusing on general vector spaces, eigenvalues/eigenvectors, inner product spaces, orthogonal matrices, and general linear transformations).
*   **Abstract Algebra** (1 Semester)  
    *   *Curriculum:* Conducted recitations covering Chapters 2–4 of Herstein's *Topics in Algebra* (focusing on Group Theory, isomorphism theorems, permutation groups, ring theory, and field theory).
