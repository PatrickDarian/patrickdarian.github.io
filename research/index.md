# Research & Thesis Progress

## Undergraduate Thesis (Defended - Pending Revisions)
* **Title:** Equivalence of 2D TQFT and Commutative Frobenius Algebras  
* **Abstract:** Investigated the categorical equivalences between the category of 2D TQFT and the category of Frobenius algebras, and providing some concrete examples.
* **Current Focus:** Adapting core results and explicit examples into a formal manuscript.
* **Thesis Advisor:** [Ryan Kasyfil Aziz, M.Si., Ph.D.](https://sites.google.com/view/ryanaziz/welcome?authuser=0)
* **Defense Committee:** [Prof. Kiki Ariyanti, M.Si., Ph.D.](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=rSzgolYAAAAJ&hl=id) and [Muhammad Imran, S.Si., M.Sc., Ph.D.](https://muh-imran.github.io/research/)
